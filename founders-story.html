Built From Lived Experience

Our why — and the ground we stand on together.

Common Ground Mental Health Charity Established September 25th 2026

Common Ground was built by someone who actively navigates mental health — not from a textbook, but from the messy, real middle of it.

People who are struggling do not need clinical lectures or judgement. They need equal ground: shared stories, practical tools, and a space where honesty is safe.

That is what we are building — a peer-support platform and upcoming charity that meets people where they are, without hierarchy, without stigma, and without the assumption that struggle makes anyone different or lesser.

Our core values

Lived Experience First

Relatable, authentic, and non-clinical. Support shaped by people who have been there.

Zero Judgment

A safe space where people can be honest without fear of being labelled or dismissed.

Equal Ground

Mental health conditions do not make anyone different or lesser. We meet as equals.

Practical Support

Actionable toolkits for low-energy days, anxiety, and the tough stretches in between.

Our 3-year roadmap

A clear path from digital peer support to nationwide, community-rooted impact.

Phase 1 · Current

Digital foundation

Building the core of Common Ground online so support is reachable from day one.

  • Digital coping toolkits live on the website
  • Website launch as a judgement-free hub
  • Launch of The Common Ground Podcast
Phase 2 · Upcoming

Community & structure

Deepening what we offer and formalising as a UK social enterprise / charity.

  • Free downloadable workbooks
  • Virtual peer-support drop-in sessions
  • Official UK CIC/CIO registration
Phase 3 · Long-term vision

Nationwide peer-led support

Partnering outward so peer support reaches more communities across the UK.

  • Partnerships with UK community organisations
  • Working with local councils and NHS hubs
  • Nationwide peer-led mental health initiatives
