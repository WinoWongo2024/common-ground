Urgent Help

If you are in crisis or need immediate support, please use the services below. You deserve real-time help from trained responders.

Important safety notice

Common Ground is a peer-support resource, not an emergency service. We cannot provide crisis intervention, medical advice, or immediate response. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services now.

Emergency 999 Emergency services — police, ambulance, fire. Use if you or someone else is in immediate danger. NHS 111 NHS 111 — medical advice and urgent care when it is not a 999 emergency. Samaritans 116 123 Free, 24/7. Confidential emotional support. You can talk about anything.
Mind Mind Infoline Information and support for mental health. Visit mind.org.uk or call 0300 123 3393 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm).
Local NHS Crisis lines Many areas have NHS mental health crisis lines. Search "NHS mental health crisis [your area]" or ask 111 for local numbers.

Other support

  • Samaritans — also available via email and self-help tools.
  • Mind — guides, local services, and advocacy.
  • NHS Mental Health — official advice and services.
