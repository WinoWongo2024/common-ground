Coping Toolkits

Practical, judgement-free strategies you can use at your own pace. Pick what feels manageable today — there is no right or wrong way.

Grounding & Anxiety Relief

Techniques to help bring you back into the present when anxiety feels overwhelming.

  • 5-4-3-2-1 groundingName 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste.
  • Box breathingBreathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat a few rounds.
  • Feet on the floorPress your feet into the ground. Notice the pressure, the texture, the temperature.
  • Cold water resetSplash cool water on your face or hold a cold drink. Notice the sensation.

Low-Energy Days

Gentle options for when getting through the day is already enough.

  • Micro-tasksOne tiny step only: open a window, drink water, text one person, change into clean clothes.
  • Self-compassion checklistWould I speak to a friend this way? Can I lower the bar today? Rest is not failure.
  • Body double (even virtual)Sit with a quiet video call, a livestream, or company in the same room while you do one small thing.
  • Permission to do lessChoose the minimum viable day. Survive first; rebuild later.

Routine & Daily Tools

Small anchors that can make days feel a little more steady over time.

  • One consistent wake-up cueSame time, same light, same drink — even if the rest of the day varies.
  • 3-item priority listWrite only three things. Anything else is bonus.
  • Check-in question"What do I need right now — rest, connection, or a small action?"
  • End-of-day wind-downDim lights, put the phone down 20 minutes earlier, one calming activity.

These are peer-shared ideas, not clinical advice. Take what helps and leave the rest. If you need urgent support, visit our Urgent Help page.
